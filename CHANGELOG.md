# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-09

Dark mode, and two layout fixes.

### New

- Dark mode that follows your phone, with an Auto, Light and Dark switch ([#32](https://github.com/n8watkins/who-am-i/pull/32))

### Fixed

- Your opponent's name fits in the phone top bar, and I Guessed It is roomier on desktop ([#36](https://github.com/n8watkins/who-am-i/pull/36))

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-09

Phone comforts: the screen stays on, you can see when your opponent steps away, sounds for the big moments, and a result card to share.

### New

- Phones keep the screen on during a game ([#26](https://github.com/n8watkins/who-am-i/pull/26))
- See when your opponent's phone is locked, and the game waits for them ([#27](https://github.com/n8watkins/who-am-i/pull/27))
- Sounds and a buzz for your turn, a correct guess and the match end ([#28](https://github.com/n8watkins/who-am-i/pull/28))
- A What's new link and the version in the footer ([#29](https://github.com/n8watkins/who-am-i/pull/29))
- Share a picture of your match result ([#30](https://github.com/n8watkins/who-am-i/pull/30))
